Products and markets

CoinGecko provides token listings and aggregate token prices. GeckoTerminal provides exact-contract pool prices, volume, liquidity and historical closes. These observations are not the underlying stock’s exchange price and do not predict what your order will receive. Pool liquidity is not a slippage guarantee.

Order quotes

USDC input ÷ equivalent share exposure
= price before network costs

Fees and price impact are reflected in output. Gas, approvals, account creation, funding, bridging, taxes and future exit costs are separate. On BNB Chain the input is Binance-Peg USDC. Funds are assumed available on the destination chain.

Conversion and identity

One token may represent more than one share. Only source-backed conversions enter equivalent-share rankings. Missing ratios stay unranked. xStocks EVM units are already adjusted; Solana uses issuer or onchain data. Other issuers do not inherit those assumptions.

Issuer security identifiers and corroborated CoinGecko underlying names, IDs, token symbols and addresses connect products. Wrapped tokens and ambiguous matches are excluded from discovery. Source labels distinguish issuer records from CoinGecko catalog mapping. The native RWA grouping can be refreshed in Sources & coverage.

Freshness and access

Market observations show source timestamps. Quotes expire after 30 seconds and must be refreshed; the venue re-quotes before a purchase. Public APIs can return partial coverage, access errors or rate limits. Missing data stays missing.

Terms summaries link primary documents. Unreviewed rights are labeled. Check product-specific terms and jurisdiction restrictions with the issuer and venue.

Stocklens does not hold funds, connect a wallet, or submit trades. Purchase links open external venues.
