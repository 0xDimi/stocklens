THE COMPARISON

Same stock. Different products.

Choose a company and an all-in budget. Stocklens groups illustrative tokenized versions and ranks the equivalent exposure that budget would buy.

How the demo calculates cost

The budget includes a modeled network fee. The remainder buys tokens at a modeled price, including spread, size-dependent price impact, and a trading fee. Tokens received are multiplied by an illustrative shares-per-token ratio.

All-in budget ÷ equivalent shares
= effective acquisition price

All numerical inputs—including reference stock prices and conversion ratios—are synthetic. Funds are assumed to be on the destination chain; bridging and funding costs are excluded. The scenarios do not establish venue availability or user eligibility.

What is real

The provider documentation linked in product details. Structural summaries were checked against those sources on October 18, 2026. Review current product-specific terms with the provider.

This demo does not connect a wallet, place trades, or recommend an investment.
