# Stocklens
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Compare tokenized representations of an underlying stock or ETF across issuers, chains and markets. Inspect token-unit market observations, actual order quotes, source-backed share conversion, pool liquidity, price history and issuer terms before following a product-specific external venue link.

## Users
People researching tokenized securities. Product access and jurisdiction eligibility are checked with issuers and venues.

## Capabilities and Constraints
CoinGecko discovery and market prices, GeckoTerminal pools and hourly charts, direct xStocks/Ondo catalogs, RPC-backed conversion and Raydium/KyberSwap quotes. Current configured directory spans 18 chains and recognizes 12 product families. Quotes cover five chains; unverified conversions stay unranked. Five views, a linked market screener, on-demand OHLC chart and volume, command palette, three-product comparison, source disclosures, filters, search, local favorites, URL state, CSV export and mobile layouts. Nasdaq public listed-security references are independently sourced and timestamped; company data loads per Markets page. No synthetic fallback, wallet, custody, transaction execution, eligibility screening, invented issuer-risk score or exhaustive-coverage claim. Home owns comparison; Markets owns the whole-catalog table. Source-native grouping loads per selected asset, beyond the fixed naming-family directory. Wrappers have distinct classification and opt-in display; coverage gaps remain measurable. Real circular company/issuer/network/venue marks are source-backed, with text fallbacks.

## Brand Commitments
Less is more. Clean code. The user prefers the earlier comparison interface: daylight surfaces, readable tables, clear primary metrics and progressive disclosure. Prioritize first-time users over terminal density. Stocklens remains a working name. The primary flow is Search NVIDIA → verified versions → compare issuers/chains → supported quotes → venue. Home and Markets are the only top-level pages; the overview adopts the supplied table composition within the daylight visual system.

## Evidence on Hand
Live issuer/CoinGecko/GeckoTerminal/RPC/quote integrations and actual browser checks on October 19, 2026 (Athens). Raw live checks retained locally. Source limits and run instructions in README.md; evidence in QA.md. Unreviewed issuer terms are labeled.

## Product Principles
- Start with the underlying asset, preserve stable identifiers.
- Separate token-unit prices from equivalent-share order costs.
- Show sources, timestamps and gaps.
- Make a product’s contracts, terms and venues easy to inspect.
