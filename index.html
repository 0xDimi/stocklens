THE COMPARISON

Same stock. Different products.

Search a company, choose a USDC input, and compare live indicative swap quotes across supported issuer deployments. Each row links to its exact contract and an external trading venue.

What the ranking measures

USDC input ÷ equivalent share exposure
= acquisition price before network costs

Swap fees and price impact are already reflected in the quoted token output. Network fees, approvals, account creation, funding, bridging, taxes, and future exit costs are excluded from ranking. Any displayed gas figure is the provider’s swap-gas estimate, not a complete transaction cost.

USDC is assumed to be available on each selected chain. On BNB Chain the quoted asset is Binance-Peg USDC. This comparison does not price moving your funds between chains or stablecoins.

Token units matter

Solana raw token outputs use verified onchain or issuer multipliers. EVM xStocks amounts are already adjusted. Ondo conversions use its public onchain data where available. A missing ratio is never assumed to be 1; those rows show token output without a normalized ranking.

Sources and freshness

Contracts come from the xStocks public API and the contract list linked in Ondo’s official documentation. Swap quotes come from Raydium and KyberSwap. Quotes become stale after 30 seconds under our freshness policy and must be refreshed. They are estimates, not reserved prices or guaranteed fills.

Legal summaries link to original sources, reviewed October 18, 2026. Product-specific terms and the provider’s eligibility checks remain authoritative.

Purchases happen on the external venue. Stocklens does not hold funds or submit trades.
