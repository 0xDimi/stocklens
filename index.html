Market observations

CoinGecko provides aggregate token prices, tokenized reference markets and exchange tickers. GeckoTerminal provides exact-contract pool prices, volume, reserves and hourly candles. These are observations, not the underlying stock’s exchange price or a size-specific order quote. Pool reserves and reported exchange depth do not guarantee slippage.

Order quotes

USDC input ÷ equivalent share exposure
= price before network costs

Fees and price impact are reflected in output. Gas, approvals, account creation, funding, bridging, taxes and future exit costs are separate. On BNB Chain the input is Binance-Peg USDC. Funds are assumed available on the destination chain.

Conversion and identity

One token may represent more than one share. Only source-backed conversions enter equivalent-share rankings. Missing ratios stay unranked. xStocks EVM units are already adjusted; Solana uses issuer or onchain data. Other issuers do not inherit those assumptions.

Issuer security identifiers and corroborated CoinGecko underlying names, IDs, symbols and addresses connect products. Wrapped tokens and ambiguous matches are excluded. Native RWA grouping can be refreshed in Sources & coverage.

Freshness and access

Quotes expire after 30 seconds. The venue re-quotes before purchase. Market feeds may be cached or partial. Universe observations cover the loaded CoinGecko market pages; missing values remain unknown. Exchange tickers cover the selected token across venues, regardless of its chain filter.

Terms summaries link issuer documents. Unreviewed rights are labeled. Check product-specific terms, venue access and jurisdiction restrictions. Stocklens opens external markets and does not hold funds or submit trades.
