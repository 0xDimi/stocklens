# Stocklens comparison workspace

Mode: Operate. The user prefers the earlier, intuitive comparison interface over a terminal. Lead with issuer products and major comparison metrics. Charts and operational detail are secondary.

All routes share one appearance setting. Dark is the default: forest canvas #091e1a, raised surface #102920, warm white #f0f6f2, secondary #a6bdb2 and mint actions #d4f5df. Light uses page #f7f8fa, white surfaces, ink #172b32 and teal #126851. src/theme.css owns semantic color roles; component CSS consumes those roles. The header sun/moon button saves the preference locally and restores it before styles paint. Unavailable storage keeps the default dark appearance; the toggle still works for the current page. Green/red indicate signed changes. System sans carries the interface; tabular numbers and occasional monospace contract addresses support accurate reading.

Layout: normal document scrolling, 1440px maximum width, 48px desktop gutters, a 248px asset finder and a flexible comparison area. No market tape, right inspector, terminal counters or persistent large chart. The company name, product/issuer/network coverage and Compare buy quotes action orient the user. The product list shows token and issuer, price per token, 24h volume, structure and networks before any row expands.

Buy comparison leads with a source-qualified best-quote summary: token/network, equivalent shares received and USDC cost per equivalent share before network costs. Expired and unverified conversions cannot appear as winners. Source timestamps and cost limits remain visible.

Progressive disclosure: product rows expand for rights, dividends, exit/access conditions and contracts. Selecting products exposes a small comparison bar. Price history and source/coverage details are collapsed below the active table; chart discovery starts on demand. Keep quote, pool and venue sources, filters, saved lists, exports and keyboard navigation accessible. Sorting and CSV export sit under More beside Refresh. On phones the repeated buy CTA and generic page introduction recede; the Buy comparison tab remains visible and the selected company leads.

Body 14px, data 13–15px, helpers 11–12px, company names 23–24px. Buttons use a consistent 7px radius. At ≤760px the asset finder collapses, tables become labeled two-column rows, and controls wrap without page overflow. Focus is visible. Motion only communicates loading and state changes; respect reduced motion.

Home, Compare and Markets are distinct navigation destinations. The logo and Home open the entry at /. Compare opens the token workspace at /?page=comparison; existing stock/ticker links remain valid. The separate multi-stock comparison remains under Markets. Markets is a full-width stock table with native filters, 25-row pagination, fixed company identity, tabular data, a shared watchlist and direct comparison links. Preserve the user's reference-table hierarchy within the selected appearance; no additional dashboard card grid.

Real corporate, issuer, network and venue images sit in circular, white-backed marks with consistent object-fit and a readable fallback. Use source-native images when available; local logo provenance lives with the assets. Named networks stay visible alongside icons; icons alone do not carry meaning.

The comparison header distinguishes a tokenized aggregate from the Nasdaq listed-stock reference, each with its own timestamp. Native-grouping status is separate from legal review. Product rows expose issuer structure and named networks. Wrappers are opt-in, visibly counted and explicitly classified. The mobile first view gives enough space for one complete product row; inline source context and the price-basis note remain compact.

Stock overviews extend the shared identity with prominent listed and tokenized prices, a representative pool premium, source-provided observed history, issuer terms and exact network contracts. Markets supports a distinct two-to-five-stock comparison. Token product selection says Select for terms; Buy quotes shows provider, actual liquidity sources, size-specific output and expiry. Compare has two primary tabs: Compare versions and Compare buy quotes. Product terms, Liquidity, Trading venues and Price basis follow the results as supporting research. The exact normalized pool price used by the premium sits next to its listed reference; the CoinGecko aggregate is a separate, dated line. Best normalized routes lead per deployment, with alternate providers and conversion-unverified quotes in disclosures. Markets offers persisted columns and optional quote/conversion filters; stock baskets survive navigation and reload.

The bare Home route is a dark forest entry with oversized warm-white/mint type, a working stock search, and a bespoke lens graphic connecting a canonical stock to actual catalog products. See docs/discover.design.md. Explicit stock, ticker and comparison-view links bypass the entry and open Compare directly. The entry inherits the product's real marks, uses dated catalog counts and adds no market-data requests. Home navigation returns to the entry; Compare and Markets have their own active navigation state.

Compare keeps verified onchain share prices independent of listed-feed availability. A current comparable normalized pool leads; a size-specific $1,000 buy comparison is a labeled fallback before unaligned raw observations. Older observations retain dates. Markets uses explicit metric widths and includes source-reported 24h turnover beside the premium; table scrolling stays within its labeled region.
